# The High Winter Elves of Girlville

An unofficial parody tabletop roleplaying game, built on **Cortex Prime**, from a
premise reported by *The Onion*: pop star Olivia Rodrigo's fantasy tabletop game
about the enchanted Frost Kingdom of Girlville — where feminine winter elves
reclaim their realm from the twelve princes who were once their lovers.

Obsession, jealousy, heartbreak. The Mines of Glimmōr. The Blizzard of
Heartbreak. Glitter, both a currency and an affliction.

## What's here

- `manuscript.html` — the full game text and layout source (single-file HTML).
- `dist/girlville-halfletter.pdf` — 16-page half-letter (5.5×8.5 in) reading PDF.
- `dist/girlville-signatures.pdf` — print-ready imposed signatures (2-up on US
  Letter, landscape).

## Printing

Print `dist/girlville-signatures.pdf` on US Letter, 100% scale, duplex,
**flip on long edge**. Fold into one 16-page booklet.

## Building

The PDFs are rendered from `manuscript.html` with
[WeasyPrint](https://weasyprint.org/). Body type is Gelasio (a metric-compatible
Georgia substitute); die glyphs use the CortexSymbology font.
